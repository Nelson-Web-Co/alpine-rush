Gear Selection Tips

  1. Board Length: Choose a board that reaches between your chin and nose when standing upright - shorter for beginners, longer for advanced riders.
  2. Boot Fit is Everything: Spend time getting properly fitted - your boots should be snug but not painful, with minimal heel lift.
  3. Start with Softer Flex: Beginners should opt for softer flexing boards and boots for easier learning and forgiveness.
  4. Invest in Quality Bindings: Good bindings transfer your movements efficiently to the board and last for years.
  5. Layer System: Use a three-layer system: base layer, insulating layer, and waterproof shell for optimal temperature control.
  6. Safety First: Never compromise on helmet and goggle quality - they protect your most valuable assets.
  7. Try Before You Buy: Rent or demo gear when possible to understand your preferences before making major purchases.
  8. Seasonal Shopping: Buy gear at the end of season for significant discounts on quality equipment.

Gear Maintenance

  • Wax your board every 5-10 days of riding for optimal performance
  • Store your board in a cool, dry place during off-season
  • Clean and dry your boots after each use to prevent odor and extend life
  • Check binding screws regularly and replace worn parts
  • Wash technical clothing according to manufacturer instructions to maintain water resistance
  • Replace goggle lenses when scratched for clear vision
  • Inspect helmet for cracks or damage after any significant impact
